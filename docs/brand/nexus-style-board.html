Nexus

Nexora's identity: the place where people, projects, time and money connect.

1. One motif: nodes joined by lines

Nexus has one shape: nodes joined by thin lines. It's used in the shell bar, page headers, the login band, progress steps, empty states and the loading mark, and nowhere else, so it stays recognizable.

Empty states use the same nodes around the icon, in the area's color.

2. The navy shell

The top bar is navy in both themes, made from blue.900 with a glow line under it. Each tab carries its area's dot, and the active tab turns white.

3. Area colors (wayfinding)

Each top area has one color, used for the tab dot, the active rail icon, the page header's side bar, the breadcrumb and empty states. These colors only show where you are. They never mean a status; statuses keep gray, blue, amber, green and red.

Rules

  • Area colors and tile colors show where and what. Status always uses its own tones with a dot or icon.
  • White icons sit only on solid colors that reach at least 3:1 against white. Text on soft backgrounds reaches 4.5:1.
  • The pattern is always faint and decorative; screen readers never see it.
  • Product code uses the tokens --nx-* and --area* in globals.css and the components in src/components/ui/nexus.tsx, never raw hex values.
