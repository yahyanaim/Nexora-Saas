## What the employee learns

- My work is the place to start the day: hours still to log, tasks sorted overdue first, vacation left.
- Hours are logged on the timesheet, task by task, and saved as soon as you leave the cell.
- Submitting the week sends it to your manager; you can no longer change it unless it is sent back.

The demo week follows today's date, so the hours shown change with the day of recording.
